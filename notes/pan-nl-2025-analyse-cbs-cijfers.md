Source ID in [[source-overview]]: S10. Written in Dutch. Dated 21 November 2025 (p. 9). PAN-NL is an advocacy organisation.

**Summary for this project:** a reanalysis of the national CBS pesticide-use statistics for 2020 and 2024 (CBS StatLine table 85130NED). It gives **no figures for gladiolus or for flower bulbs as a crop group**. Its value here is (1) recent national context, before and after the 2021 ban on mancozeb and the 2023 ban on thiophanate-methyl, and (2) pointing to the CBS table that may contain bulb-crop figures. Gladiolus figures from that table: not found in sources.

## Key findings

- CBS reported on 13 November 2025 that agriculture used a fifth less plant protection products (p. 1). PAN-NL's reanalysis confirms a fall of almost 22% in total kilograms between 2020 and 2024 (p. 1; p. 2).
- The fall is mainly due to the EU ban on the fungicide mancozeb (2021) and less paraffin oil (p. 1; p. 2). Mancozeb and thiophanate-methyl (banned 2023) together accounted for 1.347 million kg in 2020 and 0 kg in 2024 (p. 3).
- PAN-NL argues that kilograms say little about risk: some replacement substances work at grams per hectare and are highly toxic to insects (p. 2; p. 4; p. 9). **This is a hazard-based argument; the report does not assess exposure or observed effects.**
- Use of propamocarb (fungicide, mainly potatoes) rose almost 600% between 2020 and 2024 (p. 2; p. 3).
- Hectares treated with ten "very toxic" synthetic insecticides selected by PAN-NL, and the kilograms used, both rose by about 50% (p. 4; p. 5, Table 2).
- Hectares treated with PFAS pesticides (per RIVM's 2022 list) rose 63%, from 626,201 to 1,021,770 ha (sum of treatments; the same field may be counted more than once) (p. 6; p. 7, Table 3).
- Bulb-related information (from Ctgb product data, as cited by PAN-NL): esfenvalerate may be applied up to 20 times per growing cycle in flower bulbs; paraffin oil up to 25 times per cycle in flower bulbs and 12 times per cycle in "bloemknollen" (flower corms) and seed potatoes (p. 5, note to Table 2). Whether this applies to gladiolus: not stated.

## Methods and limitations

- Secondary analysis of public CBS StatLine data; no new measurements (p. 1).
- Selection of "most harmful" insecticides is PAN-NL's own; PFAS classification follows RIVM (2022) (p. 4; p. 6).
- Hectares treated are summed per substance, so totals can count the same field several times (p. 6).
- Changes in crop area also affect totals (p. 2; p. 3).

## Quality flags

- Advocacy organisation (NGO); not peer-reviewed. Figures should be checked against CBS table 85130NED directly.
- Not gladiolus-specific; no bulb-crop totals given.
- Recent data (2020 and 2024): within the PRD period.
- **Internal inconsistencies:** propamocarb 2024 given as 542,493 kg (p. 2) and 542,593 kg (p. 3). Glyphosate dose described as "iets lager" (somewhat lower) while the figures given are 1.3 kg/ha (2024) and 1.0 kg/ha (2020) (p. 4).
