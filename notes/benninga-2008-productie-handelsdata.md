Source ID in [[source-overview]]: S6. Written in Dutch, with an English summary. Published September 2008. Commissioned by the Plantenziektenkundige Dienst (PD, Plant Protection Service) (p. 7).

Page numbers below are the printed page numbers.

**Summary for this project:** this report contains **no figures on gladiolus** and **nothing on pesticides**. It is an inventory of where trade and production data for pot plants and cut flowers can be found, for plant health (phytosanitary) risk management. Its worked example is orchids (chapter 4). Its use here is as a pointer to data sources for research question 2 (imports).

## Key findings

- Scope: pot plant and cut flower subsectors only (p. 7). Bulbs in pots count as pot plants (p. 13).
- Import and export data at product level can be found in Eurostat Comex, which is based on customs data (p. 17–18).
- The Combined Nomenclature (CN, the EU customs product classification) has separate codes relevant to gladiolus (pp. 17–18):
  - 06031040: cut gladioli ("Gladiolen"), under 060310 fresh cut flowers
  - 060110 (dormant bulbs, corms and rhizomes), subdivided under 06011010 to 06011090 into hyacinth, narcissus, tulip, gladiolus and other.
- Eurostat figures include transit trade and exclude estimates for non-response and small firms. CBS (Statistics Netherlands) publishes trade data excluding transit and including estimates on StatLine, but only at 6-digit level, which is too coarse to separate gladiolus (p. 18).
- Flower auction data (VBN, Flora Holland) go down to cultivar level and split supply into import (per country) and domestic supply (p. 18; pp. 21–22).
- The CBS agricultural census (*Landbouwtelling*) and PT/HBAG crop lists shown in the report do not list gladiolus as a separate crop (p. 21, Tables 3.1 and 3.2).
- Data gaps identified include: exports of end products at species level (only for larger ornamental crops); imports of propagation material from other EU countries; imports of genetic material; supply of young plants between growers (p. 7).

## Figures and data

- None on gladiolus area, trade volumes or pesticide use.

## Methods and limitations

- Desk inventory of databases plus interviews (chapters 2–3; Annex 2).
- Data situation as of 2008; databases and organisations (for example the auctions, after the merger mentioned on p. 22) may have changed since.

## Quality flags

- Old (2008), before the 2010 focus period.
- Not about pesticides; not gladiolus-specific.
- Useful only as a pointer: CN code 06031040 (cut gladioli) and the gladiolus subdivision of CN 060110 (bulbs) could be used to look up import data in Eurostat. Actual import figures: not found in sources.
