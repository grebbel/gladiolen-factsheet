### Not obtained

- **Current authorisations per crop (Ctgb).** The Ctgb database ([toelatingen.ctgb.nl](https://toelatingen.ctgb.nl/)) is organised by product, not by crop, and refuses automated access. A current list of products authorised for gladiolus, iris, crocus or freesia was therefore not found in sources.

## Quality flags (first look, before extraction)

- **S5, S6, S7** are older than the 2010 start year in the PRD. They may still give useful historical context, and S5 is specifically about gladiolus.
- **S8, S9 and S10** come from advocacy organisations. They can be cited, but claims should be checked against primary sources where possible.
- **S11** is a municipal document: its questions come from a political party, its answers are the official position.
- **S3** is a study protocol: it describes a planned study, not its results.
- **S1, S2 and S3** cover bulb crops or agriculture in general, not gladiolus specifically.

## Background material (not cited as evidence)

- *De Gelderlander*, 9 July 2026: "Eén bol voor elke Leuthenaar: De Duffelt in actie om de bijen te redden" (news article). Useful as context, not as evidence.
- Own working documents and field observations (kept locally, not published), including personal observations on the effects of gladiolus cultivation on the local living environment. Context for the owner; not cited as evidence.
