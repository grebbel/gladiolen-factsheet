CBS background note explaining why reported pesticide **use** (farmer survey) is lower than pesticide **sales** (register of all products placed on the Dutch market). Not crop-specific; no gladiolus or Iridaceae figures. Short web page without page numbers; references are to its sections.

## Key findings

- Sales, by weight of active substance, are about twice the reported use. The use figures cover about 50% of sales in 2012, 2016 and 2020 (section "Verschillen tussen afzet en gebruik"; Table 1). CBS: the difference "kan slechts gedeeltelijk worden verklaard" (can only partly be explained).
- CBS considers the sales figures "meer compleet" (more complete) (introduction).
- Reasons CBS gives for the gap (section "Verschillen tussen afzet en gebruik"):
  - Use is surveyed only for a sample of farms and only for the 42 most-grown arable and horticultural crops (almost 85% of arable land excluding grassland). Grassland is not surveyed.
  - **Use figures cover the growing season.** "Middelen die vóór en na de teelt worden gebruikt, worden hierdoor niet altijd meegenomen in de gebruikscijfers" (products used before and after the crop are not always included), causing under-reporting.
  - With tight crop rotations it is sometimes unclear to which crop use should be assigned: the current, previous or next crop.
  - **Soil disinfection:** metam-sodium, used for wet soil disinfection, has been in the questionnaire only since 2020. Use in 2020 was no longer very high; in 2012 it was still "substantieel" (substantial).
  - Use is counted by harvest year, sales by calendar year. Spring-flowering bulbs (tulips, hyacinths, daffodils) are named as crops where part of the use can be missed for this reason.
  - Stockpiling; and non-professional use is in sales but hardly in use figures.
- **Glyphosate** (section "Ontwikkeling gebruik ten opzichte van afzet per werkzame stof"):
  - Reported use was 23% of sales in 2012 and 20% in 2020.
  - "Het gebruik van glyfosaat bij een vanggewas of groenbemester wordt niet apart uitgevraagd in de enquête" (glyphosate use on a catch crop or green manure is not asked about separately in the survey).
  - Since 2016 glyphosate may no longer be used on paved surfaces.
- **Herbicides** as a group: reported use fell from 56% of sales (2012) to 48% (2020) (Table 1).

## Methods and limitations

- Explanatory note by the statistics office; CBS states the gap cannot be fully quantified.
- National, all crops; no breakdown for bulbs or gladiolus.

## Quality flags

- Official statistics body (strong for what it says about its own methods).
- Not crop-specific; does not say how much is missed for gladiolus.

## Relevance to research questions

- **Q8 (soil disinfection):** confirms that metam-sodium was not asked about before 2020.
- **Q10 (falling kg/ha):** gives documented reasons why reported use can be lower than real use, including pre- and post-crop applications and glyphosate on catch crops or green manure. It does not say that these explain the fall for gladiolus; that link is not found in sources.
