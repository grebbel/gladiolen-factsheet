Source ID in [[source-overview]]: S13. Official statistics from the agricultural census (*Landbouwtelling*). Table last modified by CBS on 30 September 2026; downloaded through the CBS open data API on 2 November 2026, region "Nederland". Years 2000–2025 final; 2026 provisional.

CBS gives area in **are** (open field) and **m²** (under glass). Below, values are converted to hectares (1 ha = 100 are = 10,000 m²) and rounded. Exact values are in `data/cbs-80780ned-iridaceae-areaal-2000-2026.csv`.

Iridaceae crops in the table: gladiolus bulbs, iris bulbs and crocus bulbs (open field, under "Bloembollen en -knollen"), gladiolus as a cut flower (open field, under "Snijbloemen", from 2017) and freesia (under glass, cut flowers).

## Key findings

- **[gladiolus] The gladiolus bulb area fell by about 60% between 2000 and 2026**, from about 1,643 ha to 662 ha (provisional). It was about 1,118 ha in 2010 and 812 ha in 2020.
- **[gladiolus] Gladiolus is also grown as an open-field cut flower:** about 118–169 ha per year in 2017–2026.
- The number of farms with gladiolus bulbs fell from 320 (2000) to 90 (2026).
- **Related Iridaceae** (open field, bulbs): iris fell from about 675 ha (2000) to 180 ha (2026); crocus from about 628 ha to 314 ha.
- **Freesia (under glass)** fell from about 221 ha (2000) to 50 ha (2025); farms from 283 to 33.
- Meanwhile the total flower bulb area grew, from about 22,513 ha (2000) to 28,418 ha (2026). Gladiolus fell from about 7% to about 2% of the total bulb area (calculated from the same columns).

## Methods and limitations

- Agricultural census; area attributed to the farm's registered location (see [[lmb-2009-voortgangsrapportage]] on differences between CBS and the bulb inspection service BKD).
- Gladiolus cut-flower area is only available from 2017.
- Regional breakdown is available in the table but was not extracted.
- Other Iridaceae genera (for example ixia, crocosmia) are not listed separately; they may fall under "other bulb and tuber crops". Not found in sources.

## Quality flags

- Official statistics, annual, recent, crop-specific: strong.
- **Differs from BKD figures:** CBS gives about 1,643 ha of gladiolus for 2000 and 1,354 ha for 2004; BKD (in [[molema-nd-gewasafhankelijk-toedienen-gladiolen]]) gives 1,339 and 1,152 ha. S7 explains CBS figures tend to be higher because they include headlands and spray-free zones and are attributed to the postal address. The two statistics are different measures, not an error.
- The 2007 CBS total bulb area (23,655 ha) matches the CBS figure quoted in S7.
