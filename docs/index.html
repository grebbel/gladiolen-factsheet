Gladiolus and pesticides in the Netherlands

An evidence-based factsheet on pesticide use in gladiolus cultivation in the Netherlands, and its effects on the environment and human health. This page shows the project and how it is being made. The factsheet itself will appear here when it is ready.

About the project

The factsheet is written for policymakers and the general public. It starts with a plain-language summary, followed by more detailed sections. It informs; it does not advocate.

This is a personal investigation with no institutional affiliation.

Research questions

  1. How many hectares of gladiolus are grown in the Netherlands each year?
  2. Are gladioli imported into the Netherlands, and if so, how many?
  3. Which pesticides are used on gladiolus, and how much?
  4. What do monitoring data and studies show about effects on water, soil and other species?
  5. What is known about health effects and exposure for workers, residents and consumers?
  6. Which regulations apply, and how are they enforced?
  7. Where is the evidence thin, conflicting or missing?

How the evidence is handled

  • Every fact on the final page traces back to a specific source, with a page or table where possible.
  • Nothing is filled in from memory. Missing information is reported as a gap.
  • Hazard (what a substance can do) is kept separate from risk (how likely harm is at actual exposure).
  • Detecting a substance is kept separate from showing that it causes harm.
  • Each finding is labelled by strength of evidence: strong, moderate, limited or conflicting.
  • When sources disagree, both are reported.

The full rules are in CLAUDE.md, and the scope is in PRD.md.
